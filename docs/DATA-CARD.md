# Data card

**Classification:** internal portfolio test data.  
**Provenance:** Synthetic listings, slots, users and payment responses. No real payment credentials, personal data or regulated funds flow.  
**Personal or client data:** none.  
**Allowed use:** testing, demonstration and private portfolio review.  
**Disallowed inference:** real-world performance, user behavior, market return, compliance or realized business effect.  
**Known bias:** fixture design reflects the intended mechanism and may make the test easier than an external setting.
